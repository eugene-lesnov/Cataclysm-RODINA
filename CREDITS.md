# Credits and sources for Cataclysm: RODINA

Cataclysm: RODINA is derived from [Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead](https://github.com/CleverRaven/Cataclysm-DDA) at the stable `0.I-1` tag. Whales created the original game, which is developed by the CDDA community. The original authors of code, game data, and translations retain their attribution. The Git history and `data/credits/en.credits` and `data/credits/ru.credits` contain further contributor details. Authorship of RODINA changes is preserved in Git history.

Artwork and tilesets include work by individual artists. Notices in `gfx/*/tileset.txt`, including `gfx/MshockXotto+/Credit.txt`, and the in-game credits are retained. New images, sounds, fonts, and other assets need clear author and license information before they are included in distributed builds.

CDDA's main code and content are distributed under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). RODINA identifies the original project and its changes and retains attribution notices. Material derived from CDDA remains under CC BY-SA 3.0; some components have separate licenses. This attribution does not imply endorsement of RODINA by CDDA's authors.

Components with separate terms include:

- Fonts: `LICENSE-OFL-Terminus-Font.txt`, `LICENSE-Apache-Robot-Font.txt`, and the GNU Unifont notice in `LICENSE.txt`.
- Bundled libbacktrace and zstd: notices in `LICENSE.txt`.
- Dear ImGui, ImTui, Unicode pinyin, and FlatBuffers: notices in `LICENSE.txt` and the respective files under `src/third-party/`.
- Tilesets: notices alongside the assets in `gfx/`.
- Dependencies included in binary packages: their applicable licenses and notices must be reviewed for each package.

`LICENSE.txt` is the main license and notice file. This page helps locate attribution and does not replace any license terms.
